ItemKeeper Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 6, 2026

ItemKeeper is designed to help you keep track of where personal items are stored.

Data Collection

ItemKeeper does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data with the developer or third parties.

Local Storage

Information you enter in ItemKeeper, including item names, locations, notes, and optional photos, is stored locally on your device. Backup files are created only when you choose to export them.

Camera and Photos

ItemKeeper may request access to the camera or photo library so you can attach photos to saved items. These photos remain associated with your locally stored ItemKeeper data and are not uploaded to the developer.

Analytics and Tracking

ItemKeeper does not use third-party advertising, tracking, or analytics services.

Children's Privacy

ItemKeeper does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated if ItemKeeper's features or data practices change.

Contact

For privacy questions about ItemKeeper, contact: comctx@icloud.com
